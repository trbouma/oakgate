SAME FOUNDATION. YOUR CHOICE.

A custodial cloak
around Acorn.

Underneath, the safebox-acorn architecture stays the same. OakGate changes who holds the secrets and takes responsibility for operating your safebox.

With OakGate, you choose to trust a provider with your keys and recovery information. With safebox-web, the service retains no user secrets, and you take responsibility for keeping them safe.

ROOM TO GO YOUR OWN WAY

When you’re ready,
Go Independent.

The planned Go Independent flow will help you recover your safebox outside OakGate, then ask the provider to end custody and delete the information it holds about you.

The handover comes first. Your safebox remains yours to use. Deletion has limits that must be explained honestly—it cannot be independently proven.
